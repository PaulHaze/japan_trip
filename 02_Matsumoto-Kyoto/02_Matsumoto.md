
# Matsumoto — Friday to Sunday



**Base:** Iroha Grand Hotel Matsumoto Ekimae  
**Shape:** Friday midday arrival · Saturday in Matsumoto · Sunday departure south, with a Narai-juku stop pencilled in.

Loose starting plan — enough structure to work from, with room for shops, side streets and spontaneous stops.

## FRIDAY — Castle + old town + Yamameya

- **Midday — arrive at Matsumoto Station.** Drop bags at Iroha Grand Hotel; quick lunch nearby if needed.
- **Early afternoon — Matsumoto Castle.** Allow roughly two hours for the keep, grounds and moat.
- **Afternoon — wander into the old centre:** castle → Ote/Daimyocho → Yohashira Shrine → Nawate Street and the Metoba River → Nakamachi Street.
- **Along the way — browse a few shops.** Start with Kochino-ya antiques, the folk art furniture showroom or crafts around Nakamachi; no need to fit them all in.
- **Early evening — THE SAKE PUB SHINSHU MATSUMOTO.** Small Nagano sake tasting before dinner.
- **Dinner — Yamameya / 山女や.** Friday's priority: local izakaya, skewers, mountain vegetables and sake. **Book ahead.**

## SAT — Market + Nakamachi + Kaichi

- **9:20 am — head to Nakamachi for the 9:30 morning market.** The one time-sensitive morning stop in the existing notes.
- **Morning — Nakamachi shops, coffee and backstreets.** Crafts, ceramics, old storehouses and wells; pick up anything missed on Friday.
- **Lunch — Shinshu soba.** Pencil in Kobayashi Soba around the old centre.
- **Afternoon — Former Kaichi School.** Explore inside, then wander the quieter Kaichi/Joto streets and Matsumoto Shrine near the castle.
- **Later afternoon — leave open.** More old-town wandering, antiques or coffee. Ishii Miso is an optional swap if a food-focused afternoon appeals more.
- **Early evening — optional sake stop at Sake to Yuki.** MUST VISIT!
- **Dinner — Shinzan / しんざん, near Matsumoto Station.** Regional food and local sake; aim for counter seats. **Reserve.** Shizuka is the cosy backup.

## SUNDAY — Departure + Narai-juku

- **Early morning — check out and head to Matsumoto Station.** Breakfast/coffee; aim for departure around **8 am**, subject to the onward plan.
- **Provisional stop — Narai-juku.** Wander the historic main street, browse shops and have a snack or lunch.
- **Continue south through the Kiso Valley toward Kyoto.** Leave trains, stop length and any other Kiso Valley stops undecided for now.
