# Kyoto — Planning Notes

## Sword-viewing options

Alternatives to the Japanese Sword Museum in Tokyo, which is closed on Tuesday 24 November 2026. These can fit into the Kyoto stage without adding a museum visit to the Tokyo nightlife day.

### Kitano Tenmangu Treasure House — historic swords

The autumn special exhibition **“Tenjin-sama and the Toyotomi Family”** runs **31 October–6 December 2026**, covering the planned Kyoto visit. The shrine lists the **Kunihiro tachi**, donated by Toyotomi Hideyori, and a **Sukemori tachi** among its displayed treasures. Both are designated Important Cultural Properties.

- **Hours:** 10:00–16:30; last admission 16:00.
- **Admission:** ¥1,000 per adult.
- **Suggested visit:** Allow roughly 45–60 minutes for the treasure house, plus time to explore the shrine.
- **What to expect:** A smaller selection of historic swords alongside other shrine treasures, rather than the depth of a dedicated sword museum. The shrine owns many more swords than are necessarily on display; do not assume a particular blade is exhibited unless the current exhibition lists it.

**SOL recommendation:** Make this the main Kyoto substitute for the missed Tokyo museum. It offers significant historic pieces and a worthwhile shrine visit in the same outing.

[Official treasure-house exhibition details](https://kitanotenmangu.or.jp/guidance/houmotsuden/)

### Gallery Tozando + Tozando Shogoin — modern craftsmanship and antiques

**Gallery Tozando** specialises in fine Japanese swords by modern master swordsmiths, displayed for sale. Its nearby sister shop, **Tozando Shogoin**, carries antique swords, armour and martial-arts equipment. Together, they offer both the craftsmanship and historical sides of Japanese swords.

- **Tozando Shogoin address:** 24 Entomi-cho, Shogoin, Sakyo-ku, Kyoto 606-8323.
- **Shogoin's published hours:** 11:00–18:00, except summer and New Year holidays. Check Gallery Tozando's own hours separately before visiting both.
- **Location:** Near the Heian Shrine area, so potentially easy to combine with sightseeing there.
- **What to expect:** Commercial galleries/shops rather than a museum collection; the blades available to view depend on current stock. Confirm visiting arrangements if making a special journey.

**SOL recommendation:** Keep this as an optional second sword stop, especially if you want more time looking at blades after Kitano Tenmangu. The gallery focuses on modern swordmaking; Shogoin adds antique pieces.

[Gallery Tozando](https://tozando-katana.com/) · [Official Tozando shop details](https://tozando.com/en/store-en/)

**Kyoto National Museum timing:** Its galleries are closed **30 November–21 December 2026** for reinstallation, so it is not a dependable sword-viewing alternative during this Kyoto stay. [Official closure notice](https://www.kyohaku.go.jp/eng/announcements/post_261104.html)
