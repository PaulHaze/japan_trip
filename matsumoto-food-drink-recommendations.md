# Matsumoto Food & Drink Recommendations

A running shortlist for our Matsumoto stay. The priority is **friendly, local, unfussy places with atmosphere** rather than fine dining.

---

## MUST VISIT — Yamameya / 山女や

**Priority:** ★★★★★ — top of the list  
**Best night:** Friday  
**Style:** Tiny, old-school local izakaya  
**Why go:** This looks exactly like the Matsumoto experience we want — small room, locals, skewers, seasonal mountain vegetables, sake, and a proper neighbourhood feel.

### What to order
- Yakitori / grilled skewers
- Seasonal mountain vegetables
- Soba
- Local Nagano sake

### Notes
- Small and popular with locals/regulars.
- Worth **booking ahead** rather than trying to walk in.
- Previously listed as a weekday evening place, so Friday is the obvious night for us.
- This is the one to organise the evening around.

---

## Shinzan / しんざん

**Priority:** ★★★★★  
**Best night:** Saturday  
**Style:** Warm, classic Shinshu izakaya  
**Why go:** Strong local-food menu, friendly atmosphere, and the sort of place where sitting at the counter should be part of the experience.

### What to order
- Basashi — horse sashimi
- Sanzoku-yaki — local garlic/soy fried chicken
- Grilled river fish / iwana if available
- Seasonal vegetables and mushrooms
- Nozawana pickles
- Soba
- Local Nagano sake, including nigori/doburoku if available

### Notes
- Close to Matsumoto Station, so very convenient from Iroha.
- Try to get **counter seats**.
- Another one worth reserving on a Saturday.

---

## Shizuka / しづか

**Priority:** ★★★★☆  
**Style:** Old Matsumoto institution; traditional, cosy izakaya  
**Why go:** Excellent late-November option — warm wooden interior, Matsumoto folk furniture, oden and sake.

### What to order
- Oden
- Basashi
- Sanzoku-yaki
- Local sake

### Notes
- Less boisterous than Yamameya or Shinzan.
- Ideal if the weather is cold and we want a slower, cosy evening.
- Think **nostalgic local conviviality rather than loud pub**.

---

## Furinkazan / 風林火山

**Priority:** ★★★★☆  
**Style:** Lively, pubbier local izakaya  
**Why go:** A good choice if we want a more energetic night with plenty of regional food and sake.

### What to order
- Basashi
- Sanzoku-yaki
- Horuten — tempura-style offal speciality
- Shinshu sake

### Notes
- Bigger and better known than Yamameya.
- Good option for a lively evening where locals and travellers mix.
- More **taishu izakaya / people's pub** in feel.

---

# Sake & Second-Stop Places

## Sake to Yuki / 酒と雪

**Priority:** ★★★★☆  
**Style:** Characterful sake bar / izakaya  
**Why go:** The owner sounds like a character and the place seems built around conversation, sake and regulars.

### Best use
Not necessarily the main dinner. Better as:
- a pre-dinner sake stop, or
- a second venue after dinner.

### Plan
Sit at the counter and ask for a couple of interesting Nagano sakes.

---

## THE SAKE PUB SHINSHU MATSUMOTO

**Priority:** ★★★★☆  
**Style:** Shinshu sake tasting bar  
**Why go:** Excellent introduction to sake from breweries all over Nagano rather than being tied to one producer.

### Best use
- Friday around 4:30–5:30 pm before Yamameya.
- Order a small tasting flight rather than settling in for the night.

---

## Fukazake / 深酒

**Priority:** ★★★☆☆  
**Style:** More sake-focused nihonshu izakaya  
**Why go:** Good backup for a later drink if we discover we want to explore Nagano sake more seriously.

### Best use
- After dinner
- Plan B if another bar is full
- A more sake-nerdy session

---

## Matsumoto Doburoku Brewery

**Priority:** ★★★★☆  
**Style:** Doburoku brewery/bar  
**Why go:** Something a little different from standard sake — rustic, cloudy doburoku.

### Best use
An afternoon or early-evening tasting stop while wandering the centre.

# Other Local Food Options

## Monogusa

**Priority:** ★★★☆☆  
**Style:** Casual local restaurant / izakaya  
**Good for:** A broad hit of Shinshu specialities in one place.

### Look for
- Shinshu soba
- Basashi
- Sanzoku-yaki
- Nozawana
- Local sake

---

## Yamagata

**Priority:** ★★★☆☆  
**Style:** Soba / local food  
**Good for:** Lunch while wandering toward the art museum / Agata side of town.

### Look for
- Soba
- Sanzoku-yaki

---

## Kobayashi Soba

**Priority:** ★★★☆☆  
**Style:** Central soba option  
**Good for:** A straightforward soba lunch if we are still around the castle/old-town area.

---

## Asahido

**Priority:** ★★★☆☆  
**Style:** Casual vegetable-focused izakaya  
**Good for:** A lighter alternative if we've already eaten a lot of fried chicken, horse meat and soba.

### Look for
- Nagano-grown vegetables
- Seasonal dishes

---

## Ishii Miso

**Priority:** ★★★★☆ as a daytime stop  
**Style:** Traditional miso producer  
**Why go:** Good cultural/food stop while wandering east toward Agatanomori.

### Look for
- Three-year-aged miso
- Simple miso-based food
- Brewery/fermentation experience

---

## Agata Bakery

**Priority:** ★★★☆☆  
**Style:** Neighbourhood bakery  
**Why go:** Useful little local stop while exploring the Agata / Agatanomori area rather than eating only in the tourist centre.

# Suggested Two-Night Food Plan

## Friday
1. Castle
2. Wander Nawate + Nakamachi
3. **THE SAKE PUB SHINSHU** for a small tasting
4. **YAMAMEYA — MUST VISIT**
5. Optional late drink if we feel like continuing

## Saturday
1. Nakamachi morning market
2. Soba or casual lunch during the day
3. Optional **Sake to Yuki** around 4:30–5:00 pm
4. **Shinzan** for dinner
5. **Fukazake** or another sake stop afterwards if we're still going

---
