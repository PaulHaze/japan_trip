In terms of both volume and variety, Shibuya wins hands down.

Shinjuku has Warp and Atom for basic people, ZEROTOKYO for incredible scale and some homegrown culture, and Space for if you wanna see some real unique only-in-Tokyo stuff. But that’s about it.

Meanwhile Shibuya has Atom, TK, Neverland, Jumanji etc. for the basics, clubasia and WOMB for real cool shit, and even loads of smaller cutting-edge spaces like Enter and Music Bar Lion. A step further out, there’s Vent (the top spot for techno), Bonobo (awesome artsy club space in Harajuku), Batica (best spot for local hip-hop), and Aoyama Hachi (all sorts here, lots of students). Shinjuku can’t compete.
