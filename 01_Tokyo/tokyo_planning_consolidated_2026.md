## 2. How we want to travel in Tokyo

This is the key filter for choosing the final itinerary.

- Prefer **whole neighbourhoods / self-contained areas** over hopping across Tokyo for isolated sights.
- Avoid days involving multiple transfers and long cross-city treks just to see one shrine, market or shop.
- Happy to spend **half a day or a full day wandering one suburb**.
- Strong interest in:
  - older / lived-in Tokyo
  - historical suburbs and backstreets
  - Buddhist temples and shrines
  - parks and autumn colour
  - traditional shopping streets / shotengai
  - markets, antiques and flea markets
  - second-hand shops
  - vinyl / record shops
  - quirky fashion
  - old kissaten
  - local izakaya and sake
  - places with local flavour rather than tourist polish
- Prefer **not-too-bustling** neighbourhoods where possible.
- Food preference is generally **good local food rather than fine dining**, although a couple of special dinners are already in the old plan.
- Weather/foliage-dependent days should stay flexible until actual late-November conditions are clear.
