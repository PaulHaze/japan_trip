**Watch buying — what I would actually do:**

- Make a shortlist of **two or three exact references and a maximum price** before Thursday. Compare Jack Road and Kame-Kichi for the same reference, condition and included accessories, rather than treating the cheapest example of a model as automatically the best buy.
- Ask about service history, polishing, replacement dial/hands, bracelet length and spare links, box/papers, and the written warranty. On a vintage watch, originality and condition may matter more than a small price difference. Try it on before committing.
- Ask what happens if a problem appears **after you return to Australia**: where repairs happen, who pays shipping, and whether the warranty is the dealer's or the manufacturer's. Jack Road publishes its own warranty terms; do not assume every watch has worldwide manufacturer cover. [Dealer warranty terms](https://www.jackroad.co.jp/img/global/pdf/warranty_trad_en.pdf).
- Keep time for bracelet sizing, payment and paperwork. Ask about the final price politely, but don't build the budget around a discount being available.

**Important update to the tax-free advice above:** Your visit is after Japan's **1 November 2026 switch to a refund system**. At participating shops you pay the tax-inclusive price, with the consumption-tax refund following customs confirmation that the goods leave Japan. Bring your original passport, ask the dealer how its refund is paid and whether fees apply, and compare the final cost after those fees and card conversion. Keep purchases available for customs inspection at departure. This also matters for Tuesday's knife purchase; ask about the inspection procedure before checking in luggage containing it. [Japan Customs explanation](https://www.customs.go.jp/kaigairyoko/taxrefund.htm).

**BAGGAGE FORWARDING (Takkyubin / Ta-Q-Bin)**

Send your bags on **Thursday 26 November** (the day before travel) so they arrive at your Matsumoto accommodation on Friday.

**How to do it:**
1. **Thursday afternoon/evening:** Ask the hotel front desk at JR-East Hotel Mets Gotanda to send your bags via Yamato Transport (クロネコヤマト — the black cat logo). Most Tokyo hotels do this routinely for guests.
2. **Fill out the slip:** The hotel staff will help. You need:

   - Your name and the hotel's address as sender

**Do NOT send bags on Friday morning** expecting same-day delivery — Tokyo to Matsumoto is next-day delivery on the standard service. Thursday send = Friday arrival.
